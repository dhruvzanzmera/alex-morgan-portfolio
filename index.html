01 / about

The short version

I'm a frontend developer who cares about the details most people never notice — the 60fps scroll, the focus ring that actually shows up, the layout that doesn't jump while a font loads. Good interfaces are mostly invisible work.

Over the last four years I've worked with startups and product teams to turn Figma files into interfaces that hold up: responsive across devices, accessible by default, and fast enough that users never notice they're waiting.

Outside of work, I write about frontend performance, contribute to a couple of small open-source component libraries, and I'm slowly rebuilding this site every time I learn something worth showing off.
